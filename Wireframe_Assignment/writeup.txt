Website Wireframe Description

This website is designed as a personal portfolio site.

In the mobile view, all elements are arranged in a single column layout.
The logo and menu appear at the top, followed by a hero image, content
section, and footer. This layout makes it easy to scroll on smaller
screens.

In the desktop view, the layout expands horizontally. The logo and
navigation appear at the top, and the content is divided into two
columns (image and text). The footer remains at the bottom.
